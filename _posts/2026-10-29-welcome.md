Does the world need another blog? Probably not. But I often find myself lost in the search for that one website, Reddit post, or blog that I was sure I would remember. Well, I didn't remember, and the bookmarks I saved years ago are long gone.

So this is meant as a knowledge base for my side projects, which may or may not be useful for someone else as well. I'm planning to compile some things I've learned from modifying my espresso machine, repairing vintage Japanese watches, baking sourdough bread and the occasional ML hot take. Enjoy or ignore, up to you!
